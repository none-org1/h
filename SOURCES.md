## 📌 Evidentiary Grounding Principle

> **"If an official source does not explicitly establish a condition, the prototype marks it as 'Not established by the available official source' or 'Not Verified'."**

The application avoids unsupported claims, false assurances of eligibility, or speculative reasons for claim delays.
